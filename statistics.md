## What is that webpage ?

On this webpage, you can select any shooter, and display his stats and his history.
The webpage fetch all the shooter's name with the api, and display it on the selector.
You can chose a shooter and it while display his shooting history, and you can display the target with the shots of all the series of a programm.
With a programm, you can print the auto-generated stand sheet.
It while display charts of other shooting stats, (idk what but it while come.)
